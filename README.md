# PE Fund Capital Call, Distribution & NAV Model

A full fund-accounting model for a simulated $50M private equity fund — 15 LPs,
10 portfolio companies, a 3-year investment period, and 4.5 years of quarterly
activity through Q2 2026. Built to demonstrate the calculation work behind PE
fund administration: capital call schedules, a four-tier distribution
waterfall, quarterly NAV rollforwards, per-LP capital account statements,
management fee mechanics, fund IRR/MOIC, and a GP-ledger-vs-fund-administrator
variance reconciliation.

> **Synthetic fund.** The LPs, portfolio companies, valuations, and exit events
> are generated by `generate_data.py`, not drawn from a real fund. The return
> drivers (which company wins, which is written down, when exits happen) are
> authored in that file and fully visible. What this repo demonstrates is the
> **calculation machinery** — the capital calls, the waterfall, the NAV
> rollforward, the capital accounts, IRR/MOIC, and reconciliation — not a
> discovered empirical result.

## What's here

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `generate_data.py` | Generates the LP register, portfolio company register, and quarterly fair value marks / exit events. Writes `quarters.csv`, `lp_register.csv`, `portfolio_companies.csv`, `portfolio_quarterly_marks.csv`, `exit_events.csv` |
| `calculations.py` | The calculation engine: capital calls, management fees, the distribution waterfall, NAV rollforward, LP capital accounts, gross/net IRR & MOIC, and the reconciliation exercise. Writes `model_output.json` plus flat CSVs |
| `build_excel_model.py` | Builds `PE_Fund_Model.xlsx` — 15 tabs, all computed figures as **live Excel formulas** (not pasted values) |
| `PE_Fund_Model.xlsx` | The full model: assumptions, LP register, portfolio, capital calls, waterfall, NAV rollforward, per-LP capital accounts (with a dropdown-driven single-LP statement view), IRR/MOIC, and the reconciliation |
| `build_dashboard.py` | Builds `dashboard.html` from `model_output.json` |
| `dashboard.html` | Self-contained interactive dashboard — open it in a browser |
| `FUND_PERFORMANCE_ANALYSIS.md` | The write-up: fund performance, waterfall walkthrough, and the reconciliation findings |

## Run it

```bash
pip install numpy openpyxl pandas
python3 generate_data.py       # writes the LP/portfolio/marks CSVs
python3 calculations.py        # runs the model, writes model_output.json
python3 build_excel_model.py   # writes PE_Fund_Model.xlsx
python3 build_dashboard.py     # writes dashboard.html
open dashboard.html
```

## The fund

**Meridian Growth Partners III, L.P.** — $50,000,000 committed across 15 LPs
(pension funds, endowments, insurance companies, funds-of-funds, family
offices, and a sovereign wealth fund), a 3-year investment period (Q1 2022 –
Q4 2024), 10 portfolio company investments totaling $45.5M at cost, modeled
through Q2 2026 (18 quarters).

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| Management fee | 2% of committed capital during the investment period; steps down to 2% of remaining invested cost thereafter |
| Preferred return | 8% annual, compounded quarterly |
| GP catch-up | 100% to GP until GP has 20% of (preferred + catch-up) paid to date |
| Carried interest | 80% LP / 20% GP on all distributions above the catch-up |

## The Excel model

`PE_Fund_Model.xlsx` is built so every computed figure is a live formula
referencing the raw inputs (LP commitments, portfolio company cost basis,
quarterly fair value marks, exit proceeds) — change an input and the workbook
recalculates, including the distribution waterfall's cumulative quarter-over-
quarter state and the reconciliation's counterfactual waterfall re-run.
Verified against LibreOffice recalculation with zero formula errors, and the
sum of all 15 LPs' ending capital account balances ties to the fund's ending
NAV to the cent.

15 tabs: Cover, Assumptions, Quarters, LP Register, Portfolio Companies,
Portfolio Marks, Mgmt Fee Schedule, Capital Call Schedule, LP Capital Calls,
Distribution Waterfall, NAV Rollforward, LP Capital Accounts, LP Statement
(Select LP — pick any LP from a dropdown), IRR & MOIC Summary, and Variance
Reconciliation.

## Notes on method

- **Whole-fund (European) waterfall**, run cumulatively by quarter rather than
  deal-by-deal — return of capital, then preferred return, then GP catch-up,
  then the carry split, in that order, every distribution.
- **Preferred return accrues every quarter** (not just at distribution events)
  on the LP's outstanding unreturned capital balance, compounded quarterly.
- **Management fee step-down** is basis-driven: 2% of committed capital during
  the investment period, then 2% of the prior quarter's remaining invested
  cost — the same mechanic the reconciliation catches the fund administrator
  getting wrong.
- **NAV rollforward** ties by construction: unrealized gain/loss is solved as
  the plug that reconciles Beginning NAV + contributions − fees + realized
  gain − distributions to the independently-computed Ending NAV (portfolio
  fair value net of that quarter's distributions).
- **The reconciliation counterfactual is itself formula-driven** in the Excel
  model — the "fund administrator's incorrect sequencing" scenario re-runs the
  same tier logic against a modified distributable amount, not a hardcoded
  number.
